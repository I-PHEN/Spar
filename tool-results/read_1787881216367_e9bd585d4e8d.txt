   491→   491→1. The Three Session Flow Architectures
   492→   492→People present differently depending on the stakes. Your product needs three distinct flow modes, not one-size-fits-all.
   493→   493→Flow A: "The Defense" — End-of-Presentation Q&A
   494→   494→When to use: Thesis defenses, grant reviews, formal investor pitches, conference presentations.
   495→   495→How it works:
   496→   496→plain
   497→   497→Phase 1: Opening (uninterrupted)
   498→   498→  User presents slides 1-12
   499→   499→  Panel is silent, "listening" mode only
   500→   500→  Telemetry runs in background (pace, fillers) but no feedback shown
   501→   501→  
   502→   502→↓
   503→   503→
   504→   504→Phase 2: The Q&A Block (interrogation)
   505→   505→  Panel takes turns asking questions
   506→   506→  Each persona gets 2-3 questions based on their archetype
   507→   507→  User cannot advance slides — they're defending what they already showed
   508→   508→  The Skeptic attacks assumptions. The Nitpicker attacks methodology.
   509→   509→  
   510→   510→↓
   511→   511→
   512→   512→Phase 3: Closing (optional)
   513→   513→  User gets 1 minute to summarize/reframe
   514→   514→  Panel gives final challenge or acknowledges strength
   515→   515→AI Logic:
   516→   516→During Phase 1, the AI transcribes and analyzes but does NOT speak
   517→   517→It builds a "challenge queue" — a list of questions each persona will ask based on the content
   518→   518→The Skeptic's queue = claims without evidence, TAM assertions, correlation-as-causation
   519→   519→The Nitpicker's queue = methodology gaps, sample size, undefined terms
   520→   520→The So-What's queue = "so what?" moments, weak impact statements, missing competitive analysis
   521→   521→Phase 2 fires these questions in order, with follow-ups based on the user's answer quality
   522→   522→Why this matters: Some people need to get through their narrative before being challenged. Interrupting a thesis defense mid-opening would be unrealistic and frustrating.
   523→   523→Flow B: "The Board Room" — Mid-Presentation Interruption
   524→   524→When to use: Board updates, aggressive investor meetings, consulting readouts, hostile Q&As.
   525→   525→How it works:
   526→   526→plain
   527→   527→Real-time, continuous:
   528→   528→  User is on Slide 3, talking about revenue
   529→   529→  The Skeptic interrupts: "Wait — you said TAM is $50B on Slide 1. 
   530→   530→  Where did that number come from?"

   532→   532→  User attempts recovery
   533→   533→  
   534→   534→  If recovery is weak, The Nitpicker piles on: 
   535→   535→  "And you haven't defined 'active user' yet. Is that monthly or daily?"
   536→   536→  
   537→   537→  User continues to Slide 4 only if they satisfactorily answer
   538→   538→  
   539→   539→  OR: The Timekeeper cuts them off: 
   540→   540→  "You've spent 4 minutes on methodology. Move to the findings."
   541→   541→AI Logic:
   542→   542→The panel is always "armed"
   543→   543→Each persona has an "interrupt threshold" — a score that builds up as they listen
   544→   544→Threshold fills when:

   546→   546→The user uses vague methodology terms (+30% to Nitpicker)

   851→   851→    }

   855→   855→    }

   858→   858→      persona.patience -= 5;

  1176→  1176→## 6. Drill Mode Mechanics
  1177→  1177→
  1178→  1178→### Architecture
  1179→  1179→Drill Mode is a **targeted behavioral modification loop.**
  1180→  1180→User selects: "I want to drill: HEDGING"
  1181→  1181→↓
  1182→  1182→System Prompt Injection:
  1183→  1183→"You are The Skeptic. Your SOLE PURPOSE is to force the user
  1184→  1184→to stop hedging. When the user says 'I think,' 'maybe,' 'sort of,'
  1185→  1185→'kind of,' or 'probably,' you MUST immediately interrupt and say:
  1186→  1186→'Drop the qualifier. Say it directly.' Then force them to restate
  1187→  1187→without hedging. You are relentless. You do not move on until they
  1188→  1188→give you a declarative statement."
  1189→  1189→↓
  1190→  1190→Real-Time Detection Engine:
  1191→  1191→Speech-to-text streams in real-time
  1192→  1192→Regex matcher scans for hedge words
  1193→  1193→On match: TRIGGER INTERRUPT signal
  1194→  1194→↓
  1195→  1195→The Interrupt Loop:
  1196→  1196→Persona immediately stops listening and starts speaking
  1197→  1197→User's current speech is cut off
  1198→  1198→Persona delivers scripted challenge
  1199→  1199→User must respond without hedging
  1200→  1200→If they hedge AGAIN, persona interrupts AGAIN
  1201→  1201→Loop continues until clean answer
  1202→  1202→↓
  1203→  1203→Scoring (Per-Attempt):
  1204→  1204→Attempt 1 clean: 3 points
  1205→  1205→Attempt 2 clean: 2 points
  1206→  1206→Attempt 3 clean: 1 point
  1207→  1207→Still hedging: 0 points
  1208→  1208→↓
  1209→  1209→Session Debrief (Drill-Specific):
  1210→  1210→"You were challenged 14 times on hedging.
  1211→  1211→Recovered on attempt 1: 3 times
  1212→  1212→Recovered on attempt 2: 5 times
  1213→  1213→Took 3+ attempts: 6 times
  1214→  1214→Fastest recovery: 1.2 seconds
  1215→  1215→Slowest recovery: 8.4 seconds
  1216→  1216→Pattern: You hedge most when challenged on numbers."
  1217→  1217→plain
  1218→  1218→
  1219→  1219→### Drill Targets
  1220→  1220→| Drill Target | Detection Trigger | Persona Behavior | Success Criteria |
  1221→  1221→|---|---|---|---|
  1222→  1222→| **Hedging** | "I think", "maybe", "sort of", etc. | Interrupt immediately, demand restatement | Clean declarative sentence |
  1223→  1223→| **Over-explaining** | Answer >100 words for simple question | Timekeeper cuts off: "Answer in 15 words." | Answer under 15 words |
  1224→  1224→| **Recovery speed** | Pause >3 seconds after interruption | Skeptic repeats question louder | Answer starts within 2 seconds |
  1225→  1225→| **Evidence gaps** | Claims without "because", "as shown in" | Nitpicker: "What's your source?" | Cite specific evidence |
  1226→  1226→| **Burden shifting** | "What do you think?", "I'm not sure" | Skeptic: "I'm not the one presenting." | Own the answer |
  1227→  1227→| **Non-answers** | Topic pivot without addressing question | Skeptic: "You didn't answer my question." | Direct answer first |

  1231→  1231→## 7. AI Decision Engine
  1232→  1232→
  1233→  1233→### Per-Persona State Machine
  1234→  1234→```typescript
  1235→  1235→interface PersonaState {
  1236→  1236→  agitation: number;        // Interrupt threshold (0-100)
  1237→  1237→  conviction: number;       // Belief in user (0-100)
  1238→  1238→  patience: number;         // Pace tolerance (-50 to +50)
  1239→  1239→  challengeQueue: string[]; // What they're waiting to challenge
  1240→  1240→  hasFollowUp: boolean;     // Whether they have a follow-up ready
  1241→  1241→  lastDisagreement: string; // Last thing they disagreed with
  1242→  1242→}
  1243→  1243→Interrupt Decision Tree (Every 2 seconds)
  1244→  1244→plain
  1245→  1245→For each persona:
  1246→  1246→
  1247→  1247→  1. SCAN user speech for triggers
  1248→  1248→     - Claim without evidence? → agitation +20
  1249→  1249→     - Vague methodology? → agitation +25
  1250→  1250→     - No impact framing? → agitation +15
  1251→  1251→     - Hedge words (Drill Mode)? → agitation +40
  1252→  1252→     - Talking too long? → patience -10
  1253→  1253→     - Good evidence cited? → conviction +15, agitation -10
  1254→  1254→     - Direct answer? → patience +5
  1255→  1255→
  1256→  1256→  2. CHECK if agitation >= 100
  1257→  1257→     - YES → INTERRUPT
  1258→  1258→       - Pull top item from challengeQueue
  1259→  1259→       - Formulate question based on archetype
  1260→  1260→       - Reset agitation to 0
  1261→  1261→       - Set hasFollowUp = true
  1262→  1262→     - NO → Continue listening
  1263→  1263→
  1264→  1264→  3. UPDATE challengeQueue
  1265→  1265→     - Add new vulnerabilities detected
  1266→  1266→     - Remove items user addressed well
  1267→  1267→     - Prioritize by archetype specialty
  1268→  1268→Follow-Up Logic
  1269→  1269→plain
  1270→  1270→If user answers a challenge:
  1271→  1271→
  1272→  1272→  Strong answer (direct, evidence-based, concise):
  1273→  1273→    → "Okay, fair." (Skeptic acknowledges)
  1274→  1274→    → conviction +20
  1275→  1275→    → hasFollowUp = false
  1276→  1276→    → Next persona takes a turn
  1277→  1277→
  1278→  1278→  Weak answer (hedges, evades, over-explains):
  1279→  1279→    → "But you still haven't explained..." (Same persona doubles down)
  1280→  1280→    → OR: "I'd like to add..." (Another persona piles on)
  1281→  1281→    → conviction -10
  1282→  1282→    → hasFollowUp = true
  1283→  1283→
